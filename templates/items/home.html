Campus Standard Operating Procedure

How the Recovery System Works

Three straightforward steps designed to ensure belongings reach their rightful owners with 100% verification.

1. Detailed Reporting

File a report specifying the campus building, floor, date, and description. For found items, assign a secret verification question.

2. Similarity Matching

Our engine cross-checks categories, timestamps, and locations to highlight potential matches between lost and found entries in real-time.

3. Verified Handover

Claimants answer the secret verification question. Once approved, contact details unlock to safely hand over the item at security or reception.
